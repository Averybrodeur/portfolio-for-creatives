Capilano River Hatchery

This project redesigns the Capilano River Hatchery website. It helps people learn what the hatchery does and why it matters. It also makes people want to visit and explore.

Project Brief

The goal of this project was to design a responsive website that is easy to use. It explores current design trends while staying true to the brand. The new design aims to inspire a new audience to visit the Capilano River Hatchery online and in person.

Problem

The Capilano River Hatchery does not have its own website. This makes it hard to find clear and helpful information. Because of this, it may not attract a new audience or encourage people to visit.

Mobile Home Page

The hero image fades into the next section to encourage scrolling. This page helps users quickly learn about the Capilano River Hatchery and its fish through clear call-to-action buttons.

Desktop Home Page

Mobile Species Page

The tab menu helps users switch between fish quickly. The large title and image encourage scrolling. This page shares clear and simple information about the fish at the Capilano River Hatchery.
